# Sourcing Strategy

The sourcing process was done in multiple stages instead of using a single source.

## Stage 1 — Initial Discovery

I first searched broadly for:
- Hyderabad manufacturing companies
- aerospace companies in Hyderabad
- defence electronics companies
- biotech and pharma companies
- precision engineering companies
- deep-tech startups

AI tools helped generate initial company names faster.

---

## Stage 2 — Verification

After collecting names, I manually verified most companies through:
- official websites
- LinkedIn
- annual reports
- company news
- product pages
- manufacturing information

LinkedIn was especially useful for checking:
- leadership background
- company size
- engineering teams
- technical orientation

Google search was used to verify:
- recent activity
- contracts
- expansion
- product launches
- ecosystem relevance

---

## Stage 3 — Filtering

Many companies were removed during filtering because they:
- had weak technical depth
- were generic service companies
- lacked manufacturing capability
- did not fit the deep-tech thesis
- had weak long-term differentiation

The companies that remained usually had:
- strong technical capability
- engineering complexity
- scientific or industrial depth
- specialized manufacturing
- future growth potential

---

## Stage 4 — Final Structuring

The final companies were organized into sectors like:
- aerospace
- defence electronics
- biotech/pharma
- precision engineering
- industrial systems
- advanced manufacturing

This made the final dataset more structured and easier to evaluate.
