# Prompts Used During Research

The prompts changed continuously during the research process because early results were often too generic.

Some examples of prompts used:

- Hyderabad deep-tech manufacturing companies
- aerospace manufacturing companies Hyderabad
- defence electronics startups India
- biotech manufacturing Hyderabad
- founder-led engineering companies
- precision engineering companies Hyderabad
- companies working with DRDO or ISRO
- advanced manufacturing companies India
- technical moat companies Hyderabad

AI was mainly used for:
- discovering companies
- summarizing information
- identifying patterns
- speeding up initial research

But AI outputs were not trusted blindly.

For most shortlisted companies, I manually verified information using:
- Google search
- official company websites
- LinkedIn
- industry articles
- news reports

Negative prompting was also important.

I intentionally avoided:
- generic SaaS companies
- random startup lists
- marketing agencies
- low-tech businesses
- companies without engineering depth

This helped keep the final list focused on companies with strong technical and industrial capability.
