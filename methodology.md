# Methodology

I started this assignment by first trying to understand what kind of companies actually fit the DeepThought philosophy.  
At first, I assumed the research would mainly focus on biotech and pharma companies because Hyderabad is strongly associated with that ecosystem. But during the research process, I noticed that Hyderabad also has a strong presence in aerospace, defence electronics, scientific manufacturing, precision engineering, and deep-tech industrial systems.

Because of that, I shifted the research approach from “popular companies” to “high technical depth companies”.

Instead of collecting random company names, I focused on finding companies that:
- build technically complex products
- have strong engineering or scientific capability
- operate in difficult-to-replicate sectors
- have long-term industrial relevance

I used AI tools to speed up the discovery and research process, but I did not directly trust AI outputs.

For almost every company:
- I checked the official website
- searched company leadership on LinkedIn
- verified products and manufacturing capability
- checked whether the company was genuinely connected with Hyderabad
- searched recent news or reports to confirm growth and relevance

In many cases, AI suggested companies that looked impressive initially but were actually:
- generic IT service firms
- consulting businesses
- low-tech operations
- companies without real manufacturing depth

Those companies were removed manually after verification.

I also noticed that the strongest companies usually shared common patterns:
- technical founders
- scientific leadership
- advanced manufacturing
- defence/aerospace/pharma links
- high engineering complexity
- long-term relevance

After multiple rounds of filtering and verification, I created the final shortlist using six evaluation conditions:
- manufacturing depth
- Hyderabad/India ecosystem connection
- technical moat
- technical leadership
- future tailwind
- growth momentum

The final dataset reflects companies that represent Hyderabad’s deep-tech industrial ecosystem rather than just famous company names.
